SUSE Rancher is a Kubernetes management platform that enables organizations to deploy, manage, and scale Kubernetes clusters. KubeDB integrates seamlessly with SUSE Rancher, RKE2, and k3s distributions.

With KubeDB on SUSE Rancher, you can:
- Deploy and manage multiple database types across Rancher-managed clusters
- Use Rancher's multi-cluster management capabilities with KubeDB databases
- Take advantage of RKE2's lightweight yet enterprise-grade Kubernetes
- Deploy on k3s for edge and IoT use cases with full database support
- Manage all your Kubernetes and database infrastructure from a single pane of glass
