Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) is Google Cloud's managed Kubernetes service. KubeDB integrates seamlessly with Google GKE to provide fully managed database operators for your Kubernetes clusters running on Google Cloud.

With KubeDB on Google GKE, you can:
- Deploy and manage multiple database types with ease
- Leverage Google Cloud's infrastructure for reliability and performance
- Integrate with Google Cloud services for backup, monitoring, and logging
- Use GKE's auto-scaling capabilities for database workloads
